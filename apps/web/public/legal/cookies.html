Necessary cookies

The local workspace uses local_session to identify the server-managed session and local_csrf to protect state-changing requests. Both cookies are HttpOnly and SameSite=Lax. Their expiry is controlled by the server's session configuration. Signing out revokes the session; account deletion revokes all of that fixture's sessions. Optional Google sign-in binds a short-lived one-use authorization state to the same local browser cookie. Google may use its own cookies on Google's sign-in pages; those are governed by Google's policies and are not controlled by this local workspace.

Local HTTP exception

This private test is bound to HTTP loopback on this computer. Its cookies use the explicit local HTTP exception to the Secure flag. The public service is disabled; a future HTTPS deployment requires Secure session cookies and separate release review.

Other storage and tracking

The workspace does not use analytics, advertising cookies, external fonts, or browser local/session storage for chat history. Saved conversations live in the local server database. See the Privacy notice for stored content and retained minimal pseudonymous records.

Before public release

Cookie durations, domains, consent requirements, and any additional services must be verified for the final deployment and reviewed before public launch. This draft does not claim regulatory compliance.
