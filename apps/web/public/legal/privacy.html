What this local test stores

The workspace stores fixture account identifiers and display names, session records, accepted legal version, conversation titles, submitted messages, and synthetic responses on this computer. It also stores request status, estimated synthetic token counts, and minimal pseudonymous usage and deletion audit records. Pseudonymous records are not a promise of anonymity.

Where messages go

The default synthetic mode makes no external provider calls. Your test messages are not sent to an AI provider, and synthetic responses do not analyze them. Optional configured Google sign-in sends authorization and identity requests to Google. It stores the verified Google subject identifier and display name locally, with accepted-notice and session records; email addresses and Google access/refresh tokens are not saved. Google processes the sign-in under its own policies. Shared demo login is disabled in Google mode.

A separate, explicitly approved evaluation command may send only the fixed synthetic test suite to Groq. It is disabled without a concrete eligible free-only model/account scope and expiry. Its bounded captures stay in private local evaluation storage; they are not chat history, operational logs, or proof of legal clearance. Provider training, retention, region, eligibility and account settings require review for that actual test and before public use.

Deletion and retention

Deleting a conversation removes its title and messages from the active local database immediately. Account deletion removes chat content and session records and disables the fixture identity. Minimal pseudonymous usage, deletion, and audit records remain in the local test data. A production retention schedule and privileged cleanup process are not complete and must be resolved before public serving. No timed purge of these retained records is promised here. A deleted Google account leaves a one-way subject hash while retained evidence exists, preventing deletion and re-sign-in from resetting local limits. In this test environment, re-sign-in may remain unavailable until retention is resolved.

Sessions and security

Necessary cookies support server-managed sessions and protection against forged requests; see the Cookie notice. The local test uses HTTP loopback only, not public access. Shared fixture accounts do not provide private personal identity assurance. Do not use this workspace for sensitive information.

Operations and review

Operational logs must not contain raw message content, secrets, or personal information. Access to this local machine and database is controlled by its owner. Operator contact, data region, lawful basis, public retention policy, access/export procedures, incident notification, and legal compliance require review before launch.
